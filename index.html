Solar inverter replacement: quote request

Front porch wall. Single-phase supply on the United Energy network. Use the buttons on the model to switch between the existing setup and the proposed work.

Site photos

Click a camera marker on the model, or the meter box, isolators, inverter, light or front door, to see the real photo.

Work required

  1. Remove the faulty Power-One Aurora inverter from the porch wall.
  2. Supply and install a new grid-connected inverter, sized to suit the existing roof panels, on the same wall.
  3. Connect the existing panels to the new inverter through the existing solar isolators, or replace the isolators if they no longer comply.
  4. Install a new sub-board beside the meter box for the solar supply. The main switchboard has no spare space.
  5. Test, label, update the shutdown procedure and signage, and lodge the network and safety paperwork.

Please include in the quote

  • Inverter brand, model, size and warranty (options welcome).
  • Whether the existing isolators, on the wall and on the roof, can stay or need replacing.
  • Where you would put the sub-board and what goes in it.
  • Any work inside the meter box. The backing sheet looks original, so say if it needs testing before drilling.
  • Whether the new inverter needs a Wi-Fi connection to meet Victoria's emergency backstop rules.
  • Optional: a price for a battery-ready (hybrid) inverter instead.

On site now

  • Meter: i-Credit 500, single phase, owned by United Energy.
  • Main switchboard (in the meter box): normal supply main switch, RCD, circuit breakers for power, lights, oven and A/C, and a 20 A solar supply main switch. No spare poles.
  • Two isolator enclosures to the left of the inverter; one is labelled "Solar DC isolator".
  • The switchboard label says the array isolator is on the roof.
  • An energy monitor sensor is clipped to the meter. Please leave it in place.

Site notes

  • The porch light sits just above the inverter's top-left corner. Check the new inverter's clearances; the light may need moving or the inverter mounting lower.
  • The security screen door is hinged on the meter box side and opens out across the meter box. Anything new on that part of the wall needs to stay shallow (estimated under 250 mm from the brick) so the door still opens.
  • The wall is under the porch roof, out of direct rain and midday sun.
  • The pot plants under the inverter can be moved for the work.
